Import Bonus Policies via CSV

Persists Until Cleared

Upload a name-based CSV. The app matches Item Name with your Products catalog and shows a review preview. Nothing is saved until you click Apply.

Required columns: Start Date, End Date, Item Name, Target, Bonus, Policy Type, Scheme Type.
Item Name: Must match a product name in your imported Products catalog. Ambiguous names are flagged for review.
Target / Bonus: Sale quantity threshold and free bonus quantity (e.g. 13 + 1, 50 + 4).
Dates: DD-MM-YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD. Leave blank for no date limit.
Policy Type / Scheme Type: Only Bonus + Item Policy rows are imported. Discount rows are ignored.

DATA PROTECTION

Backup & recovery

Sales history is stored in this browser on this computer. Export backups regularly and keep a copy on a separate drive. Restoring a backup replaces the current Sales Analyzer database.

The database is separate from booking-session storage and the existing customer/product catalog. Clearing a booking session will not erase historical sales.
